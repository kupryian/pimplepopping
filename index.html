Privacy Policy

Pimple Popping — a game about skincare procedures
Effective date: 10 September 2026

The short version. Pimple Popping has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of ours. We never see your name, your email or your progress: everything you do in the game is written to your own device and stays there. The one thing that does leave your phone is a request for an advert — and only when you choose to watch one in exchange for a crystal. Google, which supplies those adverts, receives your advertising identifier and IP address to do it.

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Pimple Popping (the “Game”), published by P.Aleks (“we”, “us”). It applies to the Android version distributed through Google Play.

1. Information we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any account. There is no sign-in and no profile. The Game contains no analytics and no crash reporting, and we operate no servers — there is nowhere for your information to be sent to us, and nothing of yours for us to hold.

The Game does show rewarded video adverts, supplied by Google AdMob, and only when you choose to watch one. To select and deliver an advert, and to measure whether it was watched, Google collects:

  • your device's advertising identifier — a resettable ID that you can change or delete at any time in Android settings;
  • your IP address, and from it your approximate location (country or region, not a street address);
  • information about the device and the advert itself: model, operating system version, which advert was shown and for how long.

This data goes to Google, not to us. We see only anonymous totals in our AdMob account — how many adverts were watched and how much they earned. Google handles it under its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy, and its use of the advertising identifier is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

If you never open an advert, nothing leaves your device at all.

2. What the Game stores on your device

Your progress is saved locally, in the Game's own private storage:

  • the name you gave your salon;
  • your crystals, stars and visitor count;
  • which notes you have collected and which story scenes you have played;
  • your settings: language, sound and music volume, hints, tutorial.

None of this identifies you, and none of it leaves your device. It is removed completely when you uninstall the Game or clear its data through Android's settings. Because we never receive it, we cannot restore it for you — and there is nothing for us to delete on request, since uninstalling is the deletion.

3. Permissions

The Game requests vibration, for the small buzz when a procedure goes wrong, and internet access together with the permissions the advertising SDK needs to fetch an advert and read the advertising identifier.

It does not request the camera, microphone, contacts, files, precise location, or any other permission.

4. Advertising and purchases

The Game contains no in-app purchases. It is free, and there is nothing in it to buy. We never see payment information of any kind.

Advertising is limited to a single place and a single kind: a rewarded video, offered when you run out of crystals, in exchange for one crystal. You choose whether to watch it. Nothing is shown between visits, before a procedure, or anywhere else in the game, and no advert ever interrupts you.

You are never required to watch one. Crystals also return on their own — one every forty minutes — and can be traded for with stars earned by doing the work well. If you never touch an advert, you can play the whole game.

Consent. Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before any personalised advert is shown, through Google's own consent form. If you decline, or if the form cannot be shown, adverts are non-personalised — chosen without using your data. You can change your choice at any time by resetting the advertising identifier in Android settings.

5. Children

Pimple Popping is not directed to children. It depicts realistic skin procedures, including blood, and is rated accordingly in Google Play. Its target audience is adults, and it is not designed for or aimed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and adverts served in the Game are not directed to children.

6. Your rights

Data protection laws — including the GDPR in the European Economic Area, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA in California — give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability.

We ourselves hold no personal data about you: there is nothing on our side to access, export or erase, and nothing for us to sell or share. We do not sell or share personal information, for money or otherwise.

The data collected for advertising is held by Google, not by us, so requests about it are best made to Google directly through the links in section 1. Two things are entirely in your hands: you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings, and you can simply never open an advert — the game is fully playable without them.

If you believe otherwise, write to us and we will answer. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date at the top. Any change that affects what data is collected will be published before the version that makes it is released.
